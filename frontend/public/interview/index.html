📋 What to Expect

  • 1 unscored practice question to warm up
  • Scored questions adaptive to your responses
  • AI voice reads each question aloud simultaneously
  • 120 seconds preparation time per question
  • No maximum recording limit — answer fully

⏱ Answer Rules

  • Recording starts automatically when prep ends
  • You may skip prep and start early
  • Answer fully — finish early anytime
  • No maximum — stop when you are done
  • Practice question: unlimited retries

🌐 Environment

  • Quiet room, no background noise
  • Stable internet connection
  • Speak clearly and at a natural pace
  • Keep camera visible (video mode)
  • Do not switch browser tabs

📊 Evaluation Criteria

  • Technical depth and accuracy
  • Communication clarity and structure
  • Problem-solving approach
  • Confidence and delivery
  • Behavioural competency signals
⚠ Retry Policy: Practice question allows unlimited retries. Real questions cannot be retried once submitted.
